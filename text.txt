1. Center X and Y (remove mean)
        ↓
2. Compute covariance matrices:
        Cxx = X @ X.T   (n_channels × n_channels)
        Cyy = Y @ Y.T   (2*n_harmonics × 2*n_harmonics)
        Cxy = X @ Y.T   (n_channels × 2*n_harmonics)
        ↓
3. Form the matrix:
        M = inv(Cxx) @ Cxy @ inv(Cyy) @ Cyx
        ↓
4. Largest eigenvalue of M = squared maximum canonical correlation
        ↓
5. Take sqrt → correlation coefficient ρ (between 0 and 1)
